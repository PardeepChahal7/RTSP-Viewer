# Third-party notices

RTSP Viewer bundles one third-party library, unmodified, in `js/vendor/`.

## mpegts.js

- Project: https://github.com/xqq/mpegts.js (by xqq; a continuation of bilibili's flv.js)
- License: Apache License 2.0. The full license text is in the project's
  repository: https://github.com/xqq/mpegts.js/blob/master/LICENSE
- Used for: playing MPEG-TS and FLV streams in the TV's browser through Media
  Source Extensions.

The bundle itself contains the notice of the library it embeds, kept next to it
in `js/vendor/mpegts.js.LICENSE.txt`:

- es6-promise, Copyright (c) 2014 Yehuda Katz, Tom Dale, Stefan Penner and
  contributors, MIT license.
